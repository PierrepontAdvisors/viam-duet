02 · What Duet is

A robot arm that draws with you.

You make a mark. It answers back.

It starts as sketching with a robot. Then: what if Claude decides what to draw next, and draws it like Van Gogh, Mondrian, or Keith Haring? That's Duet. You make a mark. It looks, understands, and answers, in the hand of an artist you choose.

05 · Learning

You learn their language by answering back.

  • Haring

    Your blob becomes a figure.

  • Mondrian

    Your edges run out to a grid.

  • Van Gogh

    Dashes stream around your mark.

  • Your own artist
    Coming next

    Describe a style. Duet draws in it.

You pick the artist for every turn, and you pick up their language because you're answering it. Haring turns your blob into a figure. Mondrian runs your edges out to a grid. Van Gogh streams dashes around your mark. You don't study the technique. You have a conversation in it.

06 · How it works

Look. Think. Answer. Draw.

Viam under every step.

  1. Look

    The camera takes a photo of the board.

  2. Think

    Claude decides what to add.

  3. Answer

    The artist's style becomes strokes.

  4. Draw

    The Viam motion service draws them.

Under the hood it's Viam at every step. The wrist camera photographs the board. Claude reads the photo and decides what to add. The artist's style turns that into strokes. Viam's motion service draws them safely around the table.

07 · The build

One person. Two days. Claude and Viam.

Next: artists you train yourself.

▶ Play the demo

One person. Two days. Claude and Viam. And here's where it goes. My father is an architect. When I was a kid he'd take me to the diner, and while we waited for the food we'd draw together: he'd draw something, I'd draw on top of it, he'd draw again, until the food came and the drawing was done. That's how I got into art. Next, Duet learns artists you train yourself. I want to train it on my father's hand, so it can sketch with my grandchildren the way he sketched with me.
