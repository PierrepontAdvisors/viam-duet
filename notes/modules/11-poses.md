## What this page is about
Names the thing the Move exercise needs: a pose, meaning a position plus an orientation, and how it relates to the frames configured earlier.

## Key concepts
- **Pose** — x, y, z plus an orientation.
- **Orientation vector** — Viam's way of writing orientation: a direction the tool's z-axis points, plus a rotation about it (theta). In the app this appears as `ov_degrees`.
- **PoseInFrame** — a pose only means something once you say which frame it's measured in.
