## 2 · What Duet is

Yesterday morning I thought it would be fun to sketch with a robot.

Then I thought, what if Claude was the one deciding what to draw next.

And then, what if it drew like Van Gogh, or Mondrian, or Keith Haring.

That's Duet. You make a mark. It looks, understands, and answers, in the hand of an artist you choose.

---

## 5 · Learning

And you pick up the artist's language, because you're answering it.

Haring turns your blob into a figure.

Mondrian runs your edges out to a grid.

Van Gogh streams dashes around your mark.

You don't study the technique. You have a conversation in it.

---

## 6 · How it works

Under the hood, it's Viam at every step.

The wrist camera photographs the board. Claude reads it and decides. The artist's style turns that into strokes. And the motion service draws them safely around the table.

---

## 7 · The build

One person. Two days. Claude and Viam.

*[beat]*

One more thing, because I only figured this out halfway through.

When I was a kid, my father used to take me to the diner. He was an architect, so he always had a pen, and while we waited for the food we'd draw together.

He'd draw something. I'd draw on top of it. He'd draw again. Until the food came and the drawing was done.

That's how I got into art.

I didn't walk in here thinking about any of that. But somewhere in the middle of building this, it hit me.

I'd built the other side of that diner table.

*[beat]*

So. Draw one mark, and Duet answers.
